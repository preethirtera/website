Tackling the Housing Market

The housing market is notoriously complex and difficult to navigate. Aeqos exists to demystify the search process, empowering renters with transparent, actionable data through AI-driven tools.

Core AI Features

Smart Chatbot Assistant

An AI assistant guides renters through their search in plain language — answering questions, surfacing listings that fit their criteria, and synthesizing neighborhood insights like commute times, safety, and cost of living into clear, actionable summaries.

Interactive AI Map

Users set their parameters — budget, neighborhood, amenities — and the map generates in real time, plotting matching units and layering AI-scored data so trade-offs between location, price, and lifestyle are visible at a glance.

Design System

A high-contrast, brutalist system built around accessibility and bold typography: heavy borders, generous spacing, and a restrained palette that keeps dense housing data legible and calm.
